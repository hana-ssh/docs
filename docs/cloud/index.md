# Hana Cloud

Hana Cloud is optional. Sign in from Hana Desktop to keep your vaults on every device and work with other people. Data is encrypted on your device, so Hana Cloud only ever stores and relays ciphertext.

## Vault sync

Hosts, keys, snippets and port forwarding presets follow you to every device, along with your app settings. They are protected by your password and a recovery key.

## Shared vaults

Invite people by email as editors or viewers, and check each member's key fingerprint before you share. Owners can transfer a vault or remove members at any time.
